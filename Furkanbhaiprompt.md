Act as a professional Instagram Reels strategist, short-form video scriptwriter, Meta ad creative strategist, and brand storytelling expert.

I need you to write a high-converting 30-second Instagram Reel script for Budget Promotion, a Calgary-based custom apparel and promotional products company.

Brand Context:
Budget Promotion is a Calgary-based custom apparel and branded merchandise company with over 30 years of experience. They specialize in custom apparel, polos, hats, golf merch, embroidery, screen printing, branded event merchandise, and promotional products. They have worked with more than 20,000 customers and brands.

Reel Concept:
The reel should use Tiger Woods as a brand storytelling reference. The main idea is that Tiger Woods did not just wear a red polo. Over time, his Sunday red look became part of his identity, his presence, and his personal brand. This identity later evolved into his apparel brand, Sun Day Red.

The reel should connect this idea to Budget Promotion by showing that custom apparel is not just about clothing. It is about creating a recognizable presence, building identity, making teams look intentional, and helping a brand become memorable.

Important Creative Direction:
Do not sell the product first. Sell the experience.
Do not make the script sound like a basic clothing ad.
The emotional selling point should be:
“When people see your team, your event, or your company apparel, they should instantly feel your brand’s presence.”

Structure:
1. Scroll-Stopping Hook:
Start with a strong hook connected to Tiger Woods and his iconic red polo.
The hook should create curiosity and stop people from scrolling.
It should suggest that one clothing choice became much bigger than clothing.

2. Body:
Explain how Tiger Woods’ Sunday red became a visual identity, not just an outfit.
Mention Sun Day Red as the evolution of that identity into a brand.
Then transition into the core lesson:
Memorable brands do not show up randomly. They show up with consistency, intention, and a look people remember.

3. Budget Promotion Introduction:
Introduce Budget Promotion as the company that helps businesses, teams, events, and golf groups create that same kind of branded presence.
Position Budget Promotion as the go-to Calgary company for custom golf merch, custom apparel, branded polos, hats, and event merchandise.
Emphasize experience, credibility, and trust:
30 years of experience
20,000+ customers and brands
Calgary-based custom apparel and branded merchandise experts

4. CTA:
End with a conversion-focused CTA.
The CTA should push viewers to take action, request a quote, or start building their custom merch package.
The CTA should feel urgent but premium, not desperate.

Tone:
Confident, sharp, premium, and conversion-focused.
The script should feel like a short brand lesson that turns into a sales pitch.
It should sound natural when spoken by a host.
Avoid sounding too corporate or too generic.
Avoid cheesy motivational lines.
Avoid overexplaining Tiger Woods’ career.
Avoid saying “be like Tiger Woods” in a literal or unrealistic way. Instead, frame it as “build a look people remember.”

Do not imply Tiger Woods personally endorses Budget Promotion.
