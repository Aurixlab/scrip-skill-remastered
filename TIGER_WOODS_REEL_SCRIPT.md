## FINAL SCRIPT (30 seconds)

Opal Bhai stands in a golf or professional setting, holding a red polo. Slow pan to a close-up of the red fabric. Cut to: iconic Tiger Woods Sunday red moment (or symbolic red golf apparel imagery).

## 5 ALTERNATIVE HOOKS

### Alternative Hook 1 — Negative/Risk Frame
**Voice Hook:** "Most teams show up looking like they didn't plan anything. Tiger Woods showed up looking unforgettable."

**Why This Works:** Names the cost of inaction (looking unprepared), then positions the solution as the premium alternative. Hits reputation fear.

---

### Alternative Hook 2 — Curiosity/Disbelief
**Voice Hook:** "You're not going to believe what one clothing choice did for Tiger Woods' brand."

**Why This Works:** Creates open curiosity gap. Viewer watches to resolve. Soft intro that leads into the story.

---

### Alternative Hook 3 — Direct Identity Call-Out
**Voice Hook:** "If your team walks into a room and nobody notices you're there, your apparel strategy is broken."

**Why This Works:** Self-qualifies the audience (event organizers, team leaders, brands). Negative frame. Direct-to-customer tone.

---

### Alternative Hook 4 — Contrarian/Authority
**Voice Hook:** "Everyone thinks branded apparel is about the logo. Tiger Woods proved it's about the colour."

**Why This Works:** Counterintuitive insight. Positions Budget Promotion as the authority who understands what apparel really does.

---

### Alternative Hook 5 — Specificity/Premium Positioning
**Voice Hook:** "When Tiger Woods stepped onto that green in red, people didn't just see a golfer. They saw a brand."

**Why This Works:** Paints a vivid, specific moment. Premium framing. Positions apparel as a brand statement, not just clothing.

## PRODUCTION NOTES

### Presenter Delivery
- Confident, sharp tone — not rushed, not slow
- Pause slightly after "One colour became his entire identity" to let the insight land
- On the CTA, shift to conversational urgency: "today" and "now" should feel natural, not pushy
- Direct-to-camera moments should feel like you're speaking to one person, not broadcasting

### Tone Checklist
- ✓ Not corporate (authentic, conversational)
- ✓ Not cheesy (no "be like Tiger" messaging)
- ✓ Not over-explaining (Tiger Woods reference is just the hook, not the whole story)
- ✓ Sharp and premium (matches Budget Promotion's positioning)
- ✓ Conversion-focused (specific CTA, clear next step)

### Legal & Brand Safety
- Tiger Woods is used as a public brand storytelling example only — NOT an endorsement
- No claim that Budget Promotion is connected to Tiger Woods, TaylorMade, or Sun Day Red
- If using actual Tiger Woods footage, ensure proper licensing (or use symbolic red golf imagery instead)
- Budget Promotion's 30-year history and 20K customer count are real, verifiable proof points

### Testing Strategy
If performance data becomes available:
- **If hook underperforms:** Swap in Alternative Hook 1 (negative/risk frame) or Hook 3 (identity call-out)
- **If CTA underperforms:** Test "DM us [QUOTE]" keyword variant for stronger DM tracking
- **If watch time drops mid-body:** Reduce the Tiger Woods explanation to 1 line; jump faster to Budget Promotion

## WHY THIS SCRIPT WORKS

### Hook Strength
The Tiger Woods opener stops the scroll because it's **specific, unexpected, and relevant to the audience**. The insight — "one colour became his identity" — creates curiosity. Viewers want to understand how this connects to their business.

### Body Logic
The body follows the pattern: Hook (Tiger's red) → Evolution (Sun Day Red brand) → Lesson (memorable brands show up with intention) → Application (Budget Promotion helps you do this).

This is a **brand lesson disguised as a sales pitch** — which performs better than straight product ads because it educates first, sells second.

### CTA Conversion
The CTA is **specific and actionable** ("send your logo," "get started," "today") with urgency ("now"). It doesn't ask people to just "reach out" — it tells them exactly what to do next.

### Brand Alignment
This script reinforces **Budget Promotion's core promise:** Fast, reliable, in-house service backed by 30+ years and 20K customers. It positions the brand as the partner who understands that apparel is a **brand statement**, not a commodity.

### Platform Fit (Instagram Reels)
- **Hook window:** First 2 seconds are critical — Tiger Woods + identity insight lands hard ✓
- **Algorithm signals:** Curiosity hook → visual storytelling → conversion CTA = high watch completion + saves + shares ✓
- **Shareability:** The Tiger Woods analogy is shareable — people send it to team leaders, event organizers, and brand decision-makers ✓
