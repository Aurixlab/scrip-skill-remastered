# ALTERNATIVE SCRIPT 1: THE CONSEQUENCE ANGLE
## "What Happens When Your Team Shows Up Forgettable"

### Video Hook
Open on a team in mismatched, random apparel at a corporate event or job site. Camera pans to show the lack of cohesion and intentionality. Then cut to a sharp team in matching, custom branded apparel — immediate visual contrast.

### Voice Hook — MAIN
**Opal Bhai:** "Your team shows up at an event. Nobody remembers they were there."

**On-Screen Text (0:02):** "Forgettable. Invisible. Random."

---

### Body

**Opal Bhai:** "That's what happens when your apparel has no strategy. You're just... there."

**Visual Direction:** Show teams in generic, unmemorable gear. Crowd shots where the team blends in. Contrast with purposeful, cohesive branded apparel standing out.

**On-Screen Text (0:06):** "No Strategy. No Presence."

---

**Opal Bhai:** "Then you see the teams that planned it. Matching. Sharp. Intentional. People remember them."

**Visual Direction:** Cut to confident teams in custom branded apparel. They command attention. Show event attendees noticing and reacting positively to the cohesion and quality.

**On-Screen Text (0:11):** "Intentional. Memorable. Present."

---

**Opal Bhai:** "Budget Promotion helps you be the team people remember. Thirty years. Twenty thousand brands. We know how to make you stand out."

**Visual Direction:** Show Budget Promotion custom apparel — polos, hats, branded event merch, embroidery details. End on Opal Bhai or a sharp branded team.

**On-Screen Text (0:20):** "Budget Promotion | Stand Out."

---

### CTA
**Opal Bhai:** "Stop showing up invisible. Get your custom apparel started today. DM us your logo now."

**Visual Direction:** Direct-to-camera, urgent but confident delivery. Action button optional.

**On-Screen Text (0:28):** "budgetpromotion.ca | DM us"

---

### SCORECARD
- **Hook Strength:** 9/10 (Pain-point hook — visceral fear of being forgotten)
- **Body Clarity:** 9/10 (Visual contrast drives understanding)
- **CTA Sharpness:** 9/10 (Clear urgency: "stop," "today," "now")
- **Brand Alignment:** 10/10 (Positions as solution to invisibility problem)
- **Predicted Tier:** TOP (Uses PAS pattern — Problem, Agitate, Solve)

---

### WHY THIS WORKS
**Pattern:** Problem → Agitate → Solve (PAS Framework)  
**Hook Type:** Negative/Fear (being forgotten, looking unprepared)  
**Emotional Driver:** Fear of invisibility + social proof of standing out  
**CTA Type:** Hard action verb ("stop," "get started," "DM us now")  
**Differentiation:** Focuses on the **consequence of inaction** rather than the benefit of action — this hits harder for B2B/event buyers

# ALTERNATIVE SCRIPT 2: THE TRANSFORMATION ANGLE
## "From Random to Recognizable — The Budget Promotion Effect"

### Video Hook
Before shot: A team in mixed, generic apparel, no consistency, no presence. After shot: Same team (or similar team) in sharp custom branded apparel looking cohesive and professional. Quick cut between before/after 2-3 times to show the transformation.

### Voice Hook — MAIN
**Opal Bhai:** "Cheap apparel kills your brand. Quality apparel builds it."

**On-Screen Text (0:02):** "Before. After."

---

### Body

**Opal Bhai:** "See the difference? That's not just a new outfit. That's a brand upgrade."

**Visual Direction:** Side-by-side or quick-cut before/after shots. Show details: quality of fabric, fit, embroidery, colour consistency. Emphasize the visual transformation.

**On-Screen Text (0:06):** "Quality Changes Everything."

---

**Opal Bhai:** "When your team looks intentional, people trust you more. They respect you more. They remember you."

**Visual Direction:** Show reactions from event attendees, clients, customers noticing and responding positively to the branded team. Candid shots of people engaging, taking photos, being impressed.

**On-Screen Text (0:13):** "Trust. Respect. Remembered."

---

**Opal Bhai:** "That's the Budget Promotion difference. Thirty years helping brands go from forgettable to unforgettable."

**Visual Direction:** Show the full process: logo design approval, printing/embroidery close-ups, quality control, final product showcase, teams wearing with pride.

**On-Screen Text (0:20):** "Budget Promotion | From Forgettable to Unforgettable"

---

### CTA
**Opal Bhai:** "Ready to upgrade your brand? Send us your vision. We'll make it real."

**Visual Direction:** Direct-to-camera, confident and inviting. Show call-to-action button or contact info.

**On-Screen Text (0:28):** "budgetpromotion.ca | Let's upgrade your brand"

---

### SCORECARD
- **Hook Strength:** 8/10 (Before/after visual is immediately compelling)
- **Body Clarity:** 10/10 (Visual proof speaks louder than words)
- **CTA Sharpness:** 8/10 (Action verb "upgrade" + "send your vision")
- **Brand Alignment:** 9/10 (Shows the transformation Budget Promotion delivers)
- **Predicted Tier:** TOP (Uses visual hero pattern — before/after transformation)

---

### WHY THIS WORKS
**Pattern:** Before/After Transformation (from Creative Ads Guide)  
**Hook Type:** Visual contrast (problem immediately shown, then solved)  
**Emotional Driver:** Desire for upgrade + social proof from reactions  
**CTA Type:** Mid-weight (not hard-sell, but action-focused)  
**Differentiation:** Shows **tangible results** instead of just describing them — visual storytelling has higher watch completion and shares

# ALTERNATIVE SCRIPT 3: THE AUTHORITY/CREDIBILITY ANGLE
## "Why 20,000 Brands Trust Budget Promotion"

### Video Hook
Open on a montage of diverse teams and brands in Budget Promotion custom apparel: corporate teams, sports teams, event staff, restaurants, trades crews, non-profits. Quick 1-2 second clips of each showing confidence and cohesion.

### Voice Hook — MAIN
**Opal Bhai:** "Twenty thousand brands have trusted us to build their look."

**On-Screen Text (0:02):** "20,000+ Brands | 30 Years | 100% In-House"

---

### Body

**Opal Bhai:** "From boardrooms to job sites. From golf tournaments to Stampede events. Same trust. Same quality. Same commitment."

**Visual Direction:** Show montage of diverse use cases: corporate meeting, construction crew, sports team, event staff, restaurant uniform, non-profit event. Each one shows Budget Promotion apparel being worn with pride and confidence.

**On-Screen Text (0:08):** "Boardrooms. Job Sites. Events. Stampede."

---

**Opal Bhai:** "Why? Because we're in-house. We own the quality from start to finish. No middlemen. No excuses. Just results."

**Visual Direction:** Show behind-the-scenes production: embroidery machines, screen printing setup, quality control checkpoints, team members at work. Emphasize the in-house expertise.

**On-Screen Text (0:15):** "100% In-House | No Minimums | Reprint Guarantee"

---

**Opal Bhai:** "When your budget is limited and your deadline is tight, that's when Budget Promotion shows up."

**Visual Direction:** Show quick testimonial-style clips or candid moments of satisfied customers reviewing products, smiling, giving thumbs up. End on Opal Bhai or team.

**On-Screen Text (0:22):** "Same-Day Service | Price Match | Unlimited Revisions"

---

### CTA
**Opal Bhai:** "Don't gamble on your brand. Talk to the print shop that's been trusted for thirty years. Request a free quote today."

**Visual Direction:** Direct-to-camera. Confident, reassuring tone. Show quote button or contact info.

**On-Screen Text (0:28):** "Free Quote | budgetpromotion.ca"

---

### SCORECARD
- **Hook Strength:** 8/10 (Social proof is powerful — 20K brands + 30 years)
- **Body Clarity:** 9/10 (Proof points stack credibility)
- **CTA Sharpness:** 8/10 (Action verb "request," "talk to," specific offer)
- **Brand Alignment:** 10/10 (Leads with Budget Promotion's strongest differentiators)
- **Predicted Tier:** TOP (Uses authority + social proof pattern)

---

### WHY THIS WORKS
**Pattern:** Authority + Social Proof (from Creative Ads Guide #14 and testimonial patterns)  
**Hook Type:** Trust-Building / Credibility (numbers speak louder than emotion)  
**Emotional Driver:** Confidence + trust from track record + peace of mind  
**CTA Type:** Soft-to-mid (educational CTA "request free quote" appeals to consideration-stage buyers)  
**Differentiation:** Leads with **proof instead of promise** — appeals to risk-averse B2B buyers who need to feel secure in their decision

# TESTING RECOMMENDATION

**Phase 1 (Week 1):** Run all 4 scripts with equal budget  
**Phase 2 (Week 2):** Double down on top 2 performers  
**Phase 3 (Week 3):** Test hook variations on winner (use the 5 alternative hooks from original script)  

**Metrics to watch:**
- Watch completion rate (hook quality)
- Profile activity (CTA clarity)
- DM/quote volume (conversion strength)
- Shares (shareability/emotional resonance)

All 4 scripts can use **overlapping footage:**
- Custom apparel close-ups (embroidery, screen printing, quality details)
- Team montages (diverse use cases, brands in action)
- Opal Bhai talking head (record multiple takes, use for different scripts)
- On-screen text (design once, adapt for each script)

**Estimated shoot time:** 4-6 hours to capture all B-roll for all 4 scripts  
**Estimated edit time:** 2-3 hours per script (30 seconds each)
